Tips & Shortcuts

How to use
  1. Drag & drop an image onto the upload area, or click it to browse
  2. Smart Removal (default): draw a rectangle around the subject, then click Remove Background
  3. Color Match: click directly on the color to remove — processing starts automatically
  4. Adjust sliders to fine-tune; in Smart mode click Remove Background to re-run
  5. Use Erase / Restore brushes to clean up edges
  6. Click Download PNG or Copy to Clipboard
