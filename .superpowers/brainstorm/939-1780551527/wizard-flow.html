Proposed guided flow

The assistant walks the customer through these stages, one at a time. At every stage the full canvas + properties panel stay editable — the assistant only suggests and guides. Does this sequence match your vision?

1

Welcome & occasion

"What are we making today?" — gift, travel memento, collection display, milestone. Sets a friendly tone and pre-fills the project name. Skippable.

2

Choose the holdertemplate + size

Rectangle or circular holder, and overall size (in mm/inch). Maps to the existing templates & canvas sizing.

3

Pick the materialmaterial presets

Wood finish (oak, walnut…) or plastic colour. Uses the existing material/fill presets; previews on the holder live.

4

Add your coinscurrency

Choose currency (€ / $ / £), which denominations, and how many of each. Coins drop onto the holder at correct real-world diameters.

5

Arrange the layout

"Want me to tidy these into a grid / circle / rows?" One-click auto-arrange, or drag freely. Assistant suggests, never forces.

6

Personalisetext + country + shapes

Add a title/engraving, a country outline, decorative shapes or an uploaded logo.

7

Review & request

Plain-language summary of the design, then download the SVG or send a quote request (existing FormSubmit flow).

⟳ At every step the customer can ignore the assistant and edit anything directly. The assistant tracks what's on the canvas and adapts its suggestions.
